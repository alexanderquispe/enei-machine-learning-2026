\begin{frame}{How to Read This}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Machine Learning I stops at logistic regression, trees, ensembles and PCA. Everything here is \emph{discriminative $\to$ generative}: model $p(\mathbf{x}\mid y)$ and $p(y)$, then classify with Bayes' rule.
  \item Part 1 extends logistic regression to $K$ classes (softmax) and introduces LDA from the Gaussian side.
  \item Part 2 develops Gaussian discriminant analysis and the discriminative-vs-generative comparison.
  \item Part 3 is Naive Bayes for text: bag of words, the conditional-independence assumption, and closed-form estimation.
  \item Not examined in ML I. Assumed background for the text-as-data material in ML II, Lecture 5.
\end{itemize}
